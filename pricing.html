Questions About CYUH Pricing

Can I cancel my plan anytime?+
Yes, all CYUH plans are month-to-month. You can cancel or change your plan at any time with 30 days notice, without penalty.
Are there any hidden fees?+
Never. Our pricing is completely transparent. The monthly fee covers your chosen services, and any additional costs (like materials for repairs) are communicated upfront.
What if I need more services than my plan allows?+
If you exceed your plan's service limit, you can easily upgrade your plan or pay for individual services at a discounted member rate.
Do you offer a money-back guarantee?+
We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your CYUH experience within the first month, we'll refund your subscription.
