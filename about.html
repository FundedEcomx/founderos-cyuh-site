Our Story: Built for You

CYUH was founded with a simple, powerful idea: solo consumers deserve seamless, trustworthy access to local services. As busy professionals ourselves, we understand the challenge of juggling work, life, and the endless list of household tasks. Finding reliable help often felt like another chore.

That frustration sparked our mission. We set out to create a platform where quality meets convenience. CYUH meticulously vets every local expert, ensuring they meet our high standards for skill, professionalism, and reliability. This means you get peace of mind with every booking.

Today, CYUH is more than just a service platform. It's a community dedicated to giving you back your time. We believe in empowering you to focus on what truly matters, while we take care of the rest. Join us and experience the freedom of a well-managed home and more free time.
